# CTD Working Group

## Mandate
Department of Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO) National Conductivity, Temperature, and Depth Working Group (CTDWG)

The CTD Working Group (CTDWG) operates under the Ocean Science Data Management Sub-Committee (OSDMSC). Its mandate is to provide national coordination, technical guidance, and oversight for the processing, calibration, and management of Conductivity-Temperature-Depth (CTD) data across all DFO regions.

In line with DFO’s commitment to FAIR and CARE principles, the CTDWG will promote standardization, best practices, knowledge and technology sharing to improve data quality, interoperability, and reusability. 

## Scope

The scope of the CTDWG encompasses all aspects of Conductivity-Temperature-Depth (CTD) data, including additional data captured by standard CTD instrumentation. 

Activities will include: 

- Standardization of CTD Data Processing: Develop, document, and promote national standards and protocols for CTD data processing, calibration, and quality assurance. 
- Inventory and Assessment: Conduct inventories of regional CTD processing practices, tools, sensor types and data products to identify gaps, opportunities for harmonization, and best practices. 
- Technical Guidance: Provide technical advice and recommendations on CTD instrumentation, calibration protocols, data collection methods, and software tools. 
- Collaboration and Knowledge Sharing: Facilitate collaboration among regions and experts, promote sharing of expertise, and support training and capacity building in CTD data management. 
- Documentation and Reporting: Produce technical reports, templates, and documentation to support national CTD data management objectives and inform decision-making. 
- Support for Data Archival and Accessibility: Advise best practices for CTD data archival, integration with national data systems, and ensuring data accessibility and interoperability. 
- Project and Task Team Oversight: Establish and oversee task teams for specific projects related to CTD data management, as required. 
- The CTDWG will focus on current and historical CTD datasets, ensuring recommendations and standards are applicable to ongoing operations and, where feasible, apply these principles to legacy data.
